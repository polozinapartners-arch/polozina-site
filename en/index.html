How we start

From first meeting to ongoing work

Getting to know the business

At first the accountant works in your office on an agreed schedule: meets the management, learns the specifics of the company, its processes and the owner's priorities.

Accounting review

We check the state of the books, identify errors and risks, and estimate the total scope of work.

Contract

We fix the scope and the fee, and sign the contract and an NDA.

Putting the books in order

We gradually fix the books and reporting, and automate simple routine operations.

Steady ongoing work

Once the books are in order, a constant presence in the office is no longer needed: we work remotely, with every transaction under the accountant's control.

Personal help desk

For your employees we set up an AI assistant that knows the specifics of your company. It answers routine questions right away. Other questions and tasks it accepts even when the accountant is busy and passes them on as soon as the accountant is free. No request gets lost, and every answer is under the accountant's control.

Confidentiality

We work under an official contract and an NDA. Client databases are stored on our servers, and AI models also run locally on our infrastructure - the company's financial data is not shared with third parties.
